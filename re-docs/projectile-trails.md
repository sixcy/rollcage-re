# Projectile trail and launch helpers

`src/projectile-trails.mjs` reconstructs `46aba0` trail geometry, `46b000` lowering, `436870` launch-ring particles, `4354e0` nearby authored-event clearing, and `484ce0` loop-sound argument conversion. These are reusable children of the still unfinished projectile/weapon dispatcher graph.

`updateProjectileTrail(body,mode,runtime)` retains native signed ring/count fields, signed remainder32, the mode2 shrinking extent and later compensation of prior trail rows. It copies the complete matrix scratch and nine float-cache values, then executes the actual `4334b0` transforms. WORD/DWORD overlaps in private scratch are modeled directly; unused upper input words remain caller-owned rather than being zeroed. `lowerProjectileTrail(body,runtime)` preserves the one-time subtract32 behavior and live signed count.

`emitProjectileLaunchRing(position,runtime)` uses the actual free/active particle allocator, oldest-particle reuse and spatial insertion. `clearVehicleLaunchEvents(record,runtime)` is the actual behavior of `4354e0`: it clears nearby authored kind4/active1 bytes in reverse order for mode10 bodies; its initial evidence filename calling it particles was misleading. `startProjectileLoopSound(code,pitch,volume,position,velocity,runtime)` preserves unsigned16 volume and signed16 pitch.

`node tools/verify-projectile-trails-instructions.mjs` passes5,000 mixed calls,667 reached instructions and2,580,847 steps. Every mapped byte at/above `10000h` compares, including transform/float globals. Only compiler-cookie checking is supplied; all reached game helpers execute. The bank is explicitly empty for sound calls. Signed field boundaries, raw incoming stack bytes, ring/metadata overlap and particle-position aliases are included. Six focused tests pass, including exhaustion/reuse of the16-slot particle fixture and sound argument conversion. The report is `analysis/projectile-trails-instruction-check.json`.

These are synthetic body/event/arena fixtures and interpreted native comparisons. No original process was executed. Populated audio device services, complete projectile constructors, guidance/collision/expiry and the parent `46a7f0`/`47be20` graph are not established by this helper package.
