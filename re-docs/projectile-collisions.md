# Projectile collision callbacks and feedback

`src/projectile-collisions.mjs` reconstructs the three pointer callbacks installed by the actual projectile pool constructor: homing `46b820`, ground `44b6c0`, and satellite master `4302f0`. Their shared raw interface is `(body, other, event, {memory, scratchBase, ...runtime})`; `scratchBase` is the native callback entry ESP and the callbacks return zero. These are real collision policies, distinct from the `46bc30` impulse helper.

Homing detonation precedes its vehicle impact and notification, then any non-null contact marks the live owner with flag `20000h`. Ground impact and notification precede detonation and do not perform that final owner write. The actual `46bcd0` vehicle impact applies a shield effect through `482730` or adds a random angular kick, emits effects, and clears linear momentum and velocity. Other body types are untouched by that impact helper.

Satellite masters remember two object identities and suppress either repeated contact. A fresh object hit shifts that history, applies the actual impact, marks the owner, and can increment a byte score and send the actual `4724f0` score packet in modes3/8. A vehicle contact does not retire the master. A null contact with event code minus four is ignored; an ordinary world contact performs the full particle/trail/terrain/physical/spatial/audio teardown of the master and its three linked satellites. The final two satellite trails consume a high word left by the preceding native `474850` saved-register write. That real caller-stack producer is published in the reference; scratch outputs are not excluded from comparison.

`src/projectile-notifications.mjs` supplies the reached game feedback: actual `4579e0` peer/localized names, `472700` phrase formatting, `4a0620` history/queue work, guarded `4a0420` routing, and `4724f0` score-packet production. Original strings at `5d30d4` are retained in `projectile-notification-tables.json`. Literal/template variants and stale packet padding remain distinct; the only formatter boundary is the CRT `sprintf` import.

Ghidra did not recognize these callback addresses as procedures. The three `analysis/*-projectile-collision-listing.json` manifests instead retain every Listing instruction; `loadRetainedListing` independently validates their bytes and branches against original reads3353/3354/3355. The impact and score helpers also have normal retained assembly3358/3361. Target SHA256 is `c0582271862ec373f00d01545c2a95aef0abb10a32513ec585271d08b10c6cf5`.

`node tools/verify-projectile-collisions-instructions.mjs 6000` passes **6,000 calls**, **2,984 reached instructions**, **31,780,059 steps**, comparing every mapped byte at or above `10000h`, return values and ordered CRT requests. It executes4,894 actual impacts,1,401 shield effects,4,168 physical removals,4,504 trail updates,801 format calls,1,804 notification queue calls and146 score-packet producers. Fixtures have populated terrain/particles and coherent linked spatial lists; peer/localized/missing names, network gates, duplicate IDs, shield states, byte scores and callback ordering vary. Six focused tests pass. Transport interface/peer guards are deliberately closed in this checker: successful real network sends are not established by this package. Compiler cookie and CRT format/stack-probe operations are explicit services. These are interpreted-instruction comparisons over declared runtime inputs, not original-process execution.
