# Special vehicle preparation force

`src/vehicle-preparation-force.mjs` reconstructs `0x41d9a0`. The preparation parent calls it only when the complete flags word equals `0x1c03`. `applyVehiclePreparationForce(body, state, options)` mutates `body.force` and two vehicle bytes. State supplies four `{flags}` wheel records, `reverseByte64f`, `rememberedSide654`, `airCounter655`, and signed `speed664`; the speed producer remains separate and is not inferred from velocity. Options supply `gravityQ16` and explicit `timeScaleActive`, `timeScaleExempt`, `timeScaleQ16`.

Gravity is added before any early return. With no wheel contact bit0, the helper retains the remembered side and permits six calls before returning early on later calls. A wheel contact copies the full reverse byte into the remembered side and resets the airborne counter. Speed below `0x200000` produces no additional force. Higher speed is capped at `0x300000`; `(speed-0x200000)>>2` scales the signed-short rotation matrix column at indices1/4/7, negated when the selected side byte is nonzero.

Time scaling affects gravity and the speed-derived magnitude for every nonexempt call to this helper. After combining these contributions, the vector limiter compares against the unscaled global gravity and rescales through the recovered vector-length and fixed division routines. Scaling both contributions can therefore yield exactly the same limited force as an ordinary call. All products, additions and shifts retain native fixed-point rounding and signed wrap behavior.

`node tools/verify-vehicle-preparation-force-instructions.mjs` passes 3,000 fixtures over 292 retained instructions and 757,049 interpreted steps, using real vector-length, root and division callees with no helper stubs. It compares all 16 KiB of vehicle storage, including 438 airborne early-return cases, 803 fixtures above the speed cap and 1,000 scaled fixtures. Fixtures include byte edge values, threshold speeds, full signed-short matrix entries and force overflow. Six focused tests cover gravity before return, wheel bit selection, remembered side, speed threshold/cap, explicit exemption, unscaled limiter and zero gravity.

Evidence is `analysis/native-simulation/results/062-special-vehicle-force.json` and `063-special-vehicle-force-assembly.json`, with earlier math evidence listed in `analysis/vehicle-preparation-force-instruction-check.json`. These records are preserved in the offline canonical frame-preparation bundle described in [frame candidates](frame-candidates.md). Checks interpret retained instructions; no original executable process was run, and this isolated helper does not establish complete frame or race behavior.
