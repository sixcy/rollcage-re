The Object Viewer isolates models from their tracks and exports each selection with its materials and textures. Rollcage includes assembled cars with their bodies and four wheels, individual vehicle parts, and scenery. Stage II and Firebugs currently support ordinary scenery; unresolved special models are listed separately.

Select your own ROLLCAGE.IMG and ROLLCAGE.IDX files from either game's IDXData folder. The viewer identifies the supplied archives automatically.

Both files are usually located in:
Rollcage:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Psygnosis\Rollcage\IDXData
Rollcage Stage II:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Psygnosis\Rollcage Stage II\IDXData

You can paste the folder path for your game into the Browse dialog.

For Firebugs on PS1, select a disc ZIP containing its CUE and referenced data BIN. Audio tracks are skipped. Automatic identification targets the supplied Europe disc; explicitly choosing Firebugs attempts other releases with unverified compatibility.

Supports Rollcage's 20 tracks in LOW and HIGH PC versions, 66 Stage II environments, and 33 Firebugs disc environments including variants and duplicates. Firebugs environments use archive resource IDs. Stage II support targets the supplied Rollcage Extreme installation; Firebugs targets the supplied Europe disc.
