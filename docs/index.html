Rollcage & Stage II tools

Explore tracks from both games with orbit and fly-in camera controls, or download them for use in Blender.

Open the Track Viewer

Select your own ROLLCAGE.IMG and ROLLCAGE.IDX files from either game's IDXData folder. The viewer identifies the supplied archives automatically.

Both files are usually located in:
Rollcage:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Psygnosis\Rollcage\IDXData
Rollcage Stage II:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Psygnosis\Rollcage Stage II\IDXData

You can paste the folder path for your game into the Browse dialog.

Supports Rollcage's 20 tracks in LOW and HIGH PC versions, plus 66 Stage II race, scramble, training, deathmatch, pursuit, and football environments. Stage II support is validated against the installation supplied with Rollcage Extreme; other releases remain unverified.

Your game files are processed locally in your browser.
