Rollcage & Stage II track viewer

Select ROLLCAGE.IMG and ROLLCAGE.IDX from either game's IDXData folder. Both games use these filenames.

Both files are usually located in:
Rollcage:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Psygnosis\Rollcage\IDXData
Rollcage Stage II:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Psygnosis\Rollcage Stage II\IDXData

You can paste the folder path for your game into the Browse dialog.

Files are read locally and stay on your computer. You can switch games by selecting another pair of files.

Select both Rollcage.img and Rollcage.idx to begin.

Saves the complete track and ordinary objects as a ZIP with OBJ, materials and PNG textures. Unzip it, then import scene.obj into Blender.

About this reconstruction

Rollcage LOW and HIGH PC BLP tracks and Stage II environments are supported. Tracks use their archive identifiers. Stage II support is validated against the supplied installation with Rollcage Extreme. Other releases remain unverified. Terrain and ordinary objects are shown at their initial poses. Effects, overlays, special objects, game simulation and BL6 textures remain outside this viewer. Fly-in has no collision.
