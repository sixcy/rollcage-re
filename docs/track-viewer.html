Rollcage track viewer

Select Rollcage.img and Rollcage.idx. Both files are usually located in:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Psygnosis\Rollcage\IDXData

You can paste this folder path into the Browse dialog. Files are read locally and stay on your computer.

Select both Rollcage.img and Rollcage.idx to begin.

Saves the complete track and ordinary objects as a ZIP with OBJ, materials and PNG textures. Unzip it, then import scene.obj into Blender.

About this reconstruction

LOW and HIGH PC BLP packs are supported. Tracks use their archive identifiers. Terrain and ordinary objects are shown at their initial poses. Effects, overlays, special objects, game simulation and BL6 textures remain outside this viewer. Fly-in has no collision.
