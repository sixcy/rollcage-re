Rollcage, Stage II & Firebugs track viewer

Select ROLLCAGE.IMG and ROLLCAGE.IDX for Rollcage or Stage II, or a Firebugs PS1 disc ZIP containing its CUE and data BIN.

Both files are usually located in:
Rollcage:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Psygnosis\Rollcage\IDXData
Rollcage Stage II:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Psygnosis\Rollcage Stage II\IDXData

You can paste the folder path for your game into the Browse dialog.

Files are read locally and stay on your computer. Select another source to switch games. Disc loading can take a moment while the data BIN is decompressed; audio tracks are skipped.

Select both Rollcage.img and Rollcage.idx, or a Firebugs disc ZIP.

Saves the complete track and ordinary objects as a ZIP with OBJ, materials and PNG textures. Stored path overlays are viewer-only. Unzip it, then import scene.obj into Blender.

About this reconstruction

Rollcage LOW and HIGH PC BLP tracks, Stage II environments and Firebugs PS1 track resources are supported. Tracks use recovered names or archive identifiers. Stage II support targets the supplied installation with Rollcage Extreme; Firebugs targets the supplied Europe disc. Unknown releases require an explicit game selection and remain unverified. Terrain and ordinary objects are shown at their initial poses. Stored paths connect consecutive points; AI route choice and connectivity remain unresolved, and Firebugs point interpretation is experimental. Effects, runtime overlays, special objects, game simulation and BL6 textures remain outside this viewer. Fly-in has no collision. Export manifests describe source identity and reconstruction limits.
