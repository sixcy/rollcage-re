Rollcage, Stage II & Firebugs track viewer

Select ROLLCAGE.IMG and ROLLCAGE.IDX for Rollcage or Stage II, or a Firebugs PS1 disc ZIP containing its CUE and data BIN.

Both files are usually located in:
Rollcage:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Psygnosis\Rollcage\IDXData
Rollcage Stage II:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Psygnosis\Rollcage Stage II\IDXData

You can paste the folder path for your game into the Browse dialog.

Files are read locally and stay on your computer. Select another source to switch games. Disc loading can take a moment while the data BIN is decompressed; audio tracks are skipped.

About this reconstruction

Rollcage LOW and HIGH PC BLP tracks, Stage II environments and Firebugs PS1 track resources are supported. Tracks use recovered names or archive identifiers. Stage II support targets the supplied installation with Rollcage Extreme; Firebugs targets the supplied Europe disc. Unknown releases require an explicit game selection and remain unverified. Racing test is super experimental and connects recovered AI, physics, collisions, checkpoints and recovery on Rollcage tracks except LEV16 and LEV17. The car and wheels use original archive models. The test uses recovered nominal pacing and a viewer follow camera. Optional weapons use original pickup locations, award tables, AI decisions and projectile models. Continuous weapons parity is unproved; unsupported branches stop explicitly. Scenery destruction is incomplete; audio and many visual effects are omitted. Stage II and Firebugs remain static previews. Stored paths connect consecutive points; Firebugs point interpretation is experimental. Runtime overlays, special objects and BL6 textures remain outside this viewer. Fly-in has no collision. Export manifests describe static export limits.
