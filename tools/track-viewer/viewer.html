Physics progress

Racing test now has physical cameras and animated terrain, including LEV8's moving terrain points. Surface acceleration uses the recovered decay and limits.

Original-game checks now match one complete recorded six-car update, including AI, physics, attachment movement, recovery checks, drive feedback, wheel and animation updates, cameras, weather/world effects, and the final return and clock increments. This check uses recorded external clock and runtime-library results. Other checks cover selected car-to-car, car-wall and camera-border collisions, force responses and controlled respawn placement. Separate instruction checks now cover the clock calculations and step scheduling. Continuous race parity, active combat, full respawn effects and native timing validation remain under work. This remains a super experimental reconstruction.

Track editor · experimental

Left-drag brushes connected terrain. Right-drag or Shift-drag moves the camera. Release to apply; Esc cancels. Animated regions are locked. Positions use original game units. Pickups, scenery and race paths keep their saved positions and may need adjustment.

Load verified Rollcage archives to edit pickup spawn points.

Select or drag a marker. Add pickup, then click the terrain to place it. Adjust Height for clearance. Empty-space drag rotates the camera; right-drag pans. Esc cancels placement.

Move, rotate, add or remove plain scenery. Special and destructible objects are locked.

Click a model to select it; drag to move it over terrain. Adjust Height and Yaw above. Right-drag or Shift-drag controls the camera. Esc cancels. Rotation uses native 1.40625° steps.

Saving and exporting

Save project keeps your edits for later. Keep that project with its starting IMG/IDX pair. Export game files creates a new edited pair for a separate game copy. Scenery export is experimental and has not yet been validated in a running game. Your selected files stay unchanged.

About this reconstruction

Rollcage LOW and HIGH PC BLP tracks, Stage II environments and Firebugs PS1 track resources are supported. Tracks use recovered names or archive identifiers. Stage II support targets the supplied installation with Rollcage Extreme; Firebugs targets the supplied Europe disc. Unknown releases require an explicit game selection and remain unverified. Racing test is super experimental and connects recovered AI, physics, collisions, checkpoints and recovery on Rollcage tracks except LEV16 and LEV17. The car and wheels use original archive models. The test uses recovered nominal pacing and a viewer follow camera. Optional weapons use original pickup locations, award tables, AI decisions and projectile models. Continuous weapons parity is unproved; unsupported branches stop explicitly. Scenery destruction is incomplete; audio and many visual effects are omitted. Stage II and Firebugs remain static previews. Stored paths connect consecutive points; Firebugs point interpretation is experimental. Runtime overlays, special objects and BL6 textures remain outside this viewer. Fly-in has no collision. Export manifests describe static export limits.
